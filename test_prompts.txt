# GRC System Generator — Enterprise Test Scenarios

Use these scenarios to fully test the capabilities of your Opal multi-agent GRC workflow.

These prompts are intentionally detailed so the system is forced to:

* generate realistic enterprise inventories
* create meaningful risk registers
* calculate risk scores
* generate control libraries
* map evidence requirements
* produce compliance matrices
* synthesize executive dashboards
* generate policy recommendations
* produce enterprise-grade GRC reporting

The goal is to stress-test:

* multi-agent orchestration
* context retention
* cross-agent dependency handling
* structured governance generation
* enterprise realism
* compliance reasoning
* risk prioritization
* executive reporting quality

## Executive Dashboard

* governance maturity
* residual risk visualization
